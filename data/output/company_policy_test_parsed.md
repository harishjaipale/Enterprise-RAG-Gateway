NovaTech Solutions — Employee Handbook (Test Document)

# Company Overview

NovaTech Solutions is a software company founded in 2018, headquartered in Pune, India. The company specializes in building enterprise SaaS products for logistics and supply chain management. As of 2026, NovaTech employs approximately 340 people across three offices: Pune, Bengaluru, and a remote-first engineering team distributed across India.

# Work From Home Policy

Employees are permitted to work from home up to 3 days per week, subject to manager approval. Employees must be available on Slack during core hours, which are defined as 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM IST. Fully remote roles are available only for Senior Engineer level and above.

# Leave Policy

All full-time employees are entitled to 24 paid leave days per calendar year, in addition to 10 public holidays. Sick leave is capped at 12 days annually and does not carry forward to the next year. New employees become eligible for paid leave after completing 90 days of employment.

# Reimbursement Policy

Employees can claim reimbursement for internet expenses up to INR 1,500 per month, provided they work remotely at least 2 days a week. Travel for official client meetings is reimbursed at actual cost with prior approval from the reporting manager. All reimbursement claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense.

# Performance Review Cycle

NovaTech conducts performance reviews twice a year, in April and October. Reviews are based on a 5-point rating scale, and employees rated 4 or above are eligible for an annual bonus of up to 15% of their base salary. Promotions are typically announced during the October review cycle.

# IT and Security Policy

All company laptops must have disk encryption enabled and a password manager installed within the first week of onboarding. Employees are required to complete a mandatory security awareness training within 30 days of joining. Personal devices are not permitted to access the internal engineering.
