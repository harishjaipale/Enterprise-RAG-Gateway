Human Resources Policy Handbook

# 1. Employment

This handbook outlines company policies regarding employment terms, leave, conduct, and benefits for all full-time and part-time employees.

# 2. Working Hours

Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday, with flexible start times between 8:00 AM and 10:00 AM subject to manager approval.

# 3. Leave Policy

- Annual Leave: 18 days per calendar year
- Sick Leave: 12 days per calendar year
- Maternity Leave: 26 weeks as per statutory guidelines
- Paternity Leave: 15 days
- Public Holidays: as per the official company holiday calendar

# 4. Code of Conduct

Employees are expected to maintain professionalism, respect diversity, and avoid conflicts of interest. Harassment or discrimination of any kind will result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination.

# 5. Performance Reviews

Performance is reviewed twice a year (mid-year and annual) based on goal completion, peer feedback, and manager assessment.

# 6. Grievance Redressal

Employees may raise grievances confidentially through the HR portal or directly with the HR Business Partner. All complaints are investigated within 10 business days.

# 7. Exit Policy

Employees must serve a notice period of 30 days (60 days for managerial roles) and complete a formal exit interview and knowledge transfer process.
