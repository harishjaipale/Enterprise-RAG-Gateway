IT Support &#x26; Asset Policy

# 1. Purpose

This policy governs the issuance, use, and return of company IT assets, and the process for raising and resolving IT support requests.

# 2. Asset Issuance

Every employee is issued a company laptop, monitor, and standard peripherals upon joining. Assets remain company property and must be returned upon exit or role change.

# 3. Support Channels

- IT Helpdesk portal - for hardware and software issues
- Slack #it-support - for urgent, time-sensitive issues
- it-support@company.com - for asset requests and approvals

# 4. Service Level Agreements

Critical issues (system down): response within 1 hour. High priority: 4 hours. Standard requests: 1-2 business days.

# 5. Software Installation

Only software approved on the internal catalog may be installed on company devices. Requests for new software must go through IT security review.

# 6. Remote Work Equipment

Employees working remotely may request a one-time home-office equipment allowance, subject to manager and IT approval.

# 7. Asset Return

All company assets must be returned within 5 business days of the employee's last working day, in good working condition.
