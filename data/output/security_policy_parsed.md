Information Security Policy

# 1. Purpose

This policy defines the minimum security requirements for protecting company data, systems, and networks from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction.

# 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third parties who access company systems or data.

# 3. Password Requirements

- Minimum 12 characters, including upper, lower, number, and symbol
- Passwords must be rotated every 90 days
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all remote access
- Passwords must not be reused across the last 5 cycles

# 4. Data Classification

Data is classified into four levels: Public, Internal, Confidential, and Restricted. Restricted data (e.g. customer PII, financial records) must be encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.2+).

# 5. Incident Response

Any suspected security incident must be reported to the Security Team within 1 hour of discovery via the internal incident portal or security@company.com.

# 6. Access Control

Access to production systems follows the principle of least privilege and requires manager approval plus security team sign-off. Access reviews are conducted quarterly.

# 7. Policy Review

This policy is reviewed annually by the CISO office. Last reviewed: January 2026.
